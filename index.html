About This System

Hybrid AI Architecture Overview

The Hybrid AI Career Decision Support System combines two powerful AI paradigms to deliver intelligent career recommendations:

Rule-Based Engine

Uses deterministic IF-THEN rules based on academic performance, skills, and experience to make logical career decisions. Provides transparent, explainable recommendations.

Intelligent Prediction Engine

Applies weighted scoring algorithms (ML-style reasoning) to predict career suitability with confidence levels. Considers complex feature interactions.

Hybrid Fusion

Merges rule-based and prediction outputs into a unified recommendation. The hybrid approach provides both transparency and adaptive intelligence.
